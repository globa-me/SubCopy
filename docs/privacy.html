Privacy Policy for SubCopy

Effective date: August 25, 2026
Applies to: SubCopy Chrome extension version 1.2 and later

SubCopy is a local browser tool for copying YouTube subtitles, saving video thumbnails, and copying public video information. This policy explains what the extension accesses and how that information is handled.

Data collection

SubCopy does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data, browsing history, transcripts, video metadata, or downloaded images to the developers or to developer-controlled servers. The extension has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Data the extension accesses

Only after you click the SubCopy toolbar icon, the extension can access the active YouTube tab to read:

  • the current video or Short identifier;
  • available caption tracks and subtitle text;
  • public video metadata such as title, channel, duration, view count, description, chapters, links, and keywords;
  • the public thumbnail associated with the current video.

The extension processes this information in your browser. Depending on the feature and the data exposed by the YouTube page, SubCopy may request captions, metadata, or thumbnail files directly from YouTube domains. Those requests are governed by Google's and YouTube's own privacy terms. SubCopy does not send this information anywhere else.

How data is used

  • copy subtitles with or without timestamps;
  • download or copy a video thumbnail;
  • copy a Markdown summary of public video information.

SubCopy does not retain this information after the popup closes. Clipboard contents and downloaded files remain under your control and are handled by your browser and operating system.

Permissions

  • activeTab: temporary access only to the tab on which you invoke SubCopy.
  • scripting: runs the packaged extraction code in that active tab.
  • clipboardWrite: writes text or an image after you click a copy button.

SubCopy does not request persistent access to all websites or access to your browsing history.

Sharing, advertising, and human access

SubCopy does not sell or transfer user data, use it for advertising or creditworthiness, or allow the developers or other humans to read it. Its use of information is limited to the extension's user-facing single purpose, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements.

Security

All executable extension code is included in the published extension package. SubCopy does not download or execute remote code. Availability can depend on YouTube and the user's network configuration.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
